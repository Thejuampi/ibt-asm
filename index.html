02 / MAKE EVERY ADJUSTMENT COUNT

Booting is
just the start.

Your PC boots. The desktop looks fine. But can your overclock take the pressure? Give your CPU a hard workout with IBT, then use the result to decide what to try next.

01

Get your rig ready.

Check your cooling, close unnecessary apps, and keep a temperature monitor open. Your CPU is about to work hard.

02

Choose how hard to push.

Pick the stress level, run count, and CPU threads. For CPU tuning, try High · 10 runs · All threads as a starting point.

03

Test. Adjust. Go again.

Hit Start and let the test finish. A failure means those settings need another look. Adjust your setup and test again as you work toward an overclock worth keeping.

Start with a short check while you tune. Increase the run count or memory use gradually for a longer check, and keep watching temperatures. Choose your stress level ↗

03 / SEE HOW YOUR SETTINGS HOLD UP

Less guesswork.
More tuning.

Follow the test as it runs, then see where you stand: your settings passed, need another look, or haven't been fully tested yet.

PASS

Held up under pressure.

Your settings made it through every requested run without IBT detecting an error. A useful checkpoint for your next adjustment.

FAILURE

Time to retune.

IBT detected an error or the test ended unexpectedly. Revisit your settings and test again before calling the overclock done.

STOPPED

The check isn't finished.

You stopped before every requested run completed. Run the full test before counting those settings as a pass.

A pass means your settings held up during this test. Check the games and apps you use, too; no single test covers everything.
